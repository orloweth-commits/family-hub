Family Hub sounds Tap any cell to hear it.

Rows are the things that happen, columns are the looks. Each cell plays that sound exactly as the panel would at full sound level; the numbers are its length and its highest peak. “Play all” runs down one column, one sound every 0.8 s. The things on screen (record, jukebox, stamp…) sound the same in every look, only muffled under water (Sea, Reef); the boys’ own three sounds are the same everywhere.
